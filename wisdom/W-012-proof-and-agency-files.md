# W-012 · Proof before information; agency files change only from a settled task

**Date:** 2026-11-18 · **Decided by:** the owner (go to each exact wording), written by the Brain Creator ·
**Status:** Accepted. `LAW.md` v1.12 (H6), v1.13 (H2 exception), v1.14 (law 26)

**Context:** A brain carried a number recalled from a model's memory, with no bench or paper behind it.
Strong models hired for small roles drifted and redesigned what they were hired to follow. The Data
Analyst needed the owner's own chats, which H2 forbade.

**Decision:**
- **H6.** Nothing is information until a shown run, calculation or measurement, or a published paper
  with its steps, proves it. Facts are marked PROVEN or UNPROVEN.
- **H2 exception.** On the `master` and `checker` tiers the Data Analyst may read the owner's own chats
  and files that his go names for that run, names and numbers kept, outputs private and uncommitted.
- **Law 26.** Agency files (laws, brains, memory, templates, plans) change only on the owner's go, after
  a Wise Man session, or when a Commander states the task. No post redesigns them beyond its brief.

**Why, and what is measured:** McMillan (arXiv 2605.10039, abstract read, 1,650 sessions): file size,
position and layout showed no detectable effect on adherence; compliance odds fell about 5.6% per unit
of work inside a session. So law 26 works together with short briefs (laws 12, 17), and a written rule
alone is not enough: on 2026-11-17 a free-tier session read real chats although its brain said stop.
Whether law 26 itself reduces drift is UNPROVEN; the check is counting edits outside briefs.
